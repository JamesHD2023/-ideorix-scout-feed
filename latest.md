1. 🤖 This Sunday the EU's AI transparency rules go live — what it means if you use AI in your books

A date worth knowing: from 2 August, Article 50 of the EU's AI Act takes effect. AI systems that generate synthetic text, audio, images or video must mark their output as machine-detectable AI content, and deepfake-style content must be labelled. Two things worth knowing before anyone panics: the duties fall on the PROVIDERS of AI systems (the tool companies), not on individual authors — and tools that only assist with standard editing (grammar fixes, light polish that does not substantially change your text) are explicitly exempt. Systems already on the market before 2 August also get a grace period to December for the marking duty. Still, if you publish AI-assisted work into the EU market, this is the start of provenance-marking becoming normal infrastructure: the tools you use will begin labelling what they generate, and EU readers will increasingly be able to check. Deciding NOW how you would answer the "was AI involved?" question — calmly and on your own terms — beats being asked it cold later.
