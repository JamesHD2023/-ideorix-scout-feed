1. 🤖 AI labs are buying up pre-2022 print books by the lorry-load — because they can't contain AI text

The Next Web reported on 22 July that AI companies are bulk-buying physical books printed before 2022 through data broker ISBNdb — books from before the chatbot era are the last guaranteed "slop-free" training data, free of AI-generated text that degrades models trained on it. The books are destroyed in the scanning, buyers hide behind NDAs, and the broker itself admits "AI company destroys two million books" is an optics problem, suggesting clients call it "digital preservation" instead. For indie authors the practical lever is the one Ingram opened back in January: publishers who distribute through Ingram (including IngramSpark authors — you ARE the publisher) can opt out of knowing sales to AI companies. It is an imperfect shield — Ingram says only that it will make reasonable efforts — but if you are wide in print and have not filed the opt-out, this report is a good reason to decide deliberately rather than by default.
