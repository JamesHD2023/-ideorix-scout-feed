1. Closing Friday: free global programme for women in publishing — 20 places, applications end 1 August

An opportunity reminder rather than breaking news: PublisHer, the global network advancing women's leadership in publishing, is taking applications for PublisHer Studio — a free eight-week online programme starting 5 October that covers AI, business strategy and communication, with just 20 places worldwide. Applications opened 1 July and close this Friday, 1 August, so this is the last week to apply.
