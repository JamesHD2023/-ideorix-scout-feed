1. 🤖 New study: readers can't tell AI stories from human ones — and rated the AI ones higher

ALLi's news summary this week (13 August, by Dan Holloway) digs into a Villanova University study titled "Bot or Not" — over 1,600 readers guessed whether short stories were human- or AI-written, and did no better than chance. The uncomfortable second finding: in head-to-head comparisons, readers often RATED the AI-written stories higher. Coming a week after the 14,000-title study showing AI-flagged books SELL worse, the two results together frame the real question: readers can't detect it, but the market still punishes the label.
